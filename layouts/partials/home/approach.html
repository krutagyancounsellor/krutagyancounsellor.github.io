Counselling at Krutagyancounsellor

Understand yourself beyond the obvious.

Our experiences are shaped not only by who we are, but by the relationships, cultures, expectations, and systems we inhabit. These influences shape how we think, what we believe, how we relate, and the choices we perceive as possible.

My approach to counselling brings together person-centred practice and integrative psychological perspectives. I work with curiosity, critical inquiry, and an openness to different ways of understanding an experience — looking beyond immediate concerns to explore the beliefs, relational patterns, and contextual influences that may be shaping them.

Drawing on my background in educational leadership, including my work as Joint Secretary (Academics) at CBSE, I bring an interest in how people make meaning, how assumptions take hold, and how alternative perspectives can deepen understanding.

The work begins with your experience. The aim is not to prescribe answers, but to help you see more clearly, think more freely, and consider your way forward with greater understanding.
