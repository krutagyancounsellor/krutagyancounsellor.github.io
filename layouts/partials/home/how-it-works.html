How Counselling Works

A collaborative process that starts with your experience.

This is an opportunity to share, in broad terms, what has brought you here, understand how I work, and ask questions about the counselling process. It also allows us to consider whether my approach and the scope of my service align with your needs.

This is an introductory consultation, not a counselling session. It does not include therapeutic exploration or treatment, and there is no obligation to continue afterwards.

{{- /* Step 02 */ -}}

02

If you choose to proceed

If we decide that working together may be appropriate, we can discuss the practical arrangements and how counselling would begin.

The counselling process itself starts in a separate session. You can bring the concerns, questions, or experiences that feel important to you, even if they are not yet clearly defined.

{{- /* Step 03 */ -}}

03

Explore your experience in context

Our experiences do not exist in isolation. Relationships, family dynamics, cultural expectations, social environments, and personal beliefs can all influence how we understand ourselves and respond to the world.

My approach is person-centred and integrative, informed by CBT, REBT, Gestalt approaches, and Transactional Analysis.

Depending on what is relevant to your experience, we may explore beliefs, emotional responses, relational patterns, or different ways of interpreting a situation. The process is guided by your needs and perspective rather than a predetermined formula.

{{- /* Step 04 */ -}}

04

Make room for new perspectives

Understanding does not always mean finding an immediate answer. Sometimes, it begins with recognising an assumption, noticing a familiar pattern, or seeing a situation from a perspective you had not previously considered.

The work is collaborative. Your experiences, values, and choices remain central as we explore what feels meaningful and relevant to you.

The aim is not to tell you how to live or who you should become, but to support a more considered understanding of your experiences and the possibilities available to you.
