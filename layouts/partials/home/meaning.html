Sometimes, the question isn't just what is happening. It's what it means to you.

A change in your life can unsettle more than your circumstances. It can challenge the way you have understood yourself.

The role that once gave you a sense of purpose may begin to feel restrictive. Expectations you have always tried to meet may conflict with what you want for yourself. Beliefs that once seemed unquestionable may no longer explain how you feel.

Making sense of these moments can mean looking beyond the immediate situation.

How have the roles you've occupied shaped your sense of self? Which expectations have you accepted, and which have you never had the opportunity to question? How do family, culture and relationships influence what feels possible, acceptable or right?

At Krutagyancounsellor, these questions can become part of the counselling conversation.

My approach brings together a person-centred foundation and perspectives informed by different psychological frameworks. The focus is not on fitting your experience into a predetermined explanation, but on exploring its meaning within the context of your life.

Because understanding yourself sometimes begins with questioning what you have always taken for granted.
