When life changes, so can your sense of self.

Sometimes, change is not just about what is happening around you. It is also about how you understand yourself within it.

A familiar role may begin to feel restrictive. Expectations you've always lived by may no longer feel right. Relationships may shift, or you may find yourself questioning beliefs you once held without a second thought.

You might be:

  • Reconsidering your roles

    Understanding who you are beyond the responsibilities and expectations you've carried.

  • Navigating change

    Making sense of transitions in relationships, work, family life or the stage of life you're entering.

  • Questioning familiar beliefs

    Exploring what you have inherited, what you still believe in, and what may no longer fit.

  • Finding your own perspective

    Understanding your choices and experiences while making room for the influence of family, culture and relationships.

You don't need to have a clear answer to any of these questions. You can begin with what feels uncertain.
