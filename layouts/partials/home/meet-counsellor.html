Understanding people in the context of their lives.

I hold a Postgraduate Diploma in Counselling and Psychotherapy and am currently pursuing postgraduate studies in Psychology. My wider academic and professional background includes an M.Phil. in English, a B.Ed., an Executive General Management Programme from IIM Bangalore and 15 years of professional experience.

My work in education and leadership, alongside 14 years of parenting, has shaped my appreciation of how relationships, family dynamics, responsibilities and social expectations influence the way we understand ourselves and navigate change. These experiences inform my interest in identity, life transitions and the different contexts in which people make sense of their lives.

My counselling approach is person centred and integrative, informed by my ongoing engagement with psychological literature, reading circles, workshops and training. Professional supervision and personal therapy are also important parts of my continuing development as a counsellor. They support reflection, self awareness and a considered approach to working with each person's individual experiences.

At Krutagyancounsellor, I offer a space to explore what you are experiencing, question assumptions that may no longer fit, and consider how your relationships, beliefs and circumstances have shaped your perspective. You do not need to arrive with a clear explanation or a defined goal. We can begin with what feels important to you.

If you are navigating a life transition, reconsidering familiar roles or trying to understand yourself in a new way, a first conversation can help you explore whether this approach is right for you.
