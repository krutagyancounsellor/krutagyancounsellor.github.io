Meet the counsellor

Counselling that considers the whole person.

My work is grounded in the understanding that our experiences cannot always be separated from the relationships, environments, and cultural expectations that shape our lives.

Through a person-centred, integrative approach, I offer individuals a structured, confidential space to reflect on their experiences, understand their responses, and explore the questions that matter to them.

The process is collaborative, respecting each person's perspective, values, and autonomy.
